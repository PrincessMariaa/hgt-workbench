AN AUDITABLE FIRST MODEL

Every assumption has a place.

A transparent probability model for a defined donor, recipient, exposure, and inheritance endpoint.

01

The event we count

A surviving recipient founder retains a donor sequence through the chosen division horizon. Chromosomal integration, extrachromosomal maintenance, and LINE-1-mediated insertion are separate pathways; they are never added together here.

Retention is conditional on a surviving lineage. The survival layer handles loss of lineages separately. Expression, efflux, and drug resistance are not equated with inheritance.

q = p₁ × p₂ × p₃ × p₄ × p₅ × p₆

Every gate is conditional on earlier gates succeeding in the same system. The RNA route includes productive machinery access followed by a coupled reverse-transcription/insertion gate; ordinary mRNA is not assumed to behave like competent LINE-1 cargo.

02

Exposure and population

m = rate × WT:KO ratio × hours / 24
p = 1 − exp(−m × q)
E[founders] = N × p × smodified

m is an effective mean number of EV–recipient encounters. Encounters are assumed Poisson; recipients are independent within each modeled state. The rate is not a particle count added to a well.

The reference curve uses the same culture context and gate values, with drug multipliers, polyploid mixture, and selection disabled. The curve sweeps actual encounters, isolating later effects from the current encounter multiplier.

03

Stress is a mechanism hypothesis

Choosing doxorubicin or cisplatin loads evidence and an editable profile. Each starts at 1×. No drug concentration is converted into an automatic effect or a recommended exposure.

The hypothetical-shift button sets 3× encounter supply and 2× establishment, with modified survival 0.8 and unmodified survival 0.2. These are demonstration numbers, not experimental estimates. Locked gates remain unchanged.

Fold changes multiply probabilities, capped at one. A 0× modifier can close a gate. Different stresses can increase material supply while reducing survival.

04

Locks and uncertainty

A lock holds a gate exactly fixed: its value cannot be edited, drug and polyploid modifiers bypass it, and it is excluded from uncertainty sampling and reset. Each pathway keeps its own values and locks.

An unlocked gate is sampled uniformly in log₁₀ probability across its chosen ± range, bounded to positive probabilities up to 1; an exact zero stays zero. These independent, assumed distributions produce a sensitivity envelope, not an empirical confidence or Bayesian credible interval.

800 deterministic draws are used. Culture inputs, fold changes, survival, and the polyploid fraction are fixed during sampling. Correlations and structural uncertainty are not quantified.

05

Polyploidy and enrichment

Reference-state and polyploid recipients are separate classes. Each class first gets its own per-cell acquisition probability. The two probabilities are then weighted by the polyploid fraction, avoiding averaging per-encounter rates before nonlinear conversion.

Relative establishment and retention affect only polyploid recipients. They start at 1×; genome doubling never automatically doubles HGT.

Modified fraction = p sm g / [p sm g + (1−p) su]

g is relative post-selection expansion. It changes the final fraction, not the founder count or chance that a founder exists. Detection is an independent probability per surviving founder, incorporating your sampling and assay sensitivity.

06

What to bring from your experiment

  • A reliable recipient identity and donor-specific sequence to track.
  • Effective encounter or uptake measurements with a clear denominator.
  • Stress-specific survival of modified and unmodified recipient lineages.
  • A retention probability measured at the selected division horizon.
  • Evidence separating chromosomal integration from persistent extrachromosomal DNA.

This first version does not simulate changing donor numbers, spatial contacts, fusion, contact-dependent DNA transfer, pharmacodynamics, continuous treatment timing, or within-clone segregation. A cell-free or co-culture signal alone does not assign an EV mechanism.

A note for the Pgp WT → KO experiment

Measure genetic acquisition and the Pgp phenotype as distinct outputs. Functional protein transfer, donor-cell carryover, fusion, and recipient adaptation are alternative explanations for Pgp positivity or survival. A donor-specific sequence retained in a verified KO lineage supports acquisition; mapped recipient–donor junctions support chromosomal integration. Source:

Changing the division horizon updates the event definition. It does not extrapolate a retention curve; set the retention gate for that horizon. This prototype has no fitted parameters and should not be interpreted as an experimentally validated HGT rate.
